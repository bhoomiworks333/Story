# "Nǐ hǎo" reel

Instagram Reel edit of a home video (child asks an AI voice assistant why we say "nǐ hǎo"; answer: "Meeha!").

    ./render.sh <source.mp4>        # writes ../output/<source name>_final.mp4, never touches the source

- Audio: highpass 80 Hz → lowpass 12 kHz → arnndn (GregorR `sh.rnnn`, mix 0.5) → afftdn nf=-25 → two-pass loudnorm -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP.
  Full-strength arnndn cut the laptop (AI) voice by ~6 dB and gated ~23 % of its frames, hence mix 0.5.
- Subtitles (`subs.ass`, generated by `make_ass.py`): Montserrat Bold, black on white boxes. The source has Hindi
  captions burned in; the white "Cover" rectangles hide them for every frame they are on screen.
- The hook emoji is drawn by libass in monochrome only, so it is made invisible in the .ass and `emoji.png`
  (Noto Color Emoji) is overlaid at the same spot for 0–4 s.
- Timings came from speech onsets in the cleaned audio, speaker pitch (child ~290–365 Hz, AI ~170 Hz) and the source's
  Hindi captions; faster-whisper could not be used (model host blocked in the build environment).
- Push-in 100 → 108 % from 16.9 s, 1 s freeze-frame with silence at the end.
