Kal raat white car
kiski thi?
...achha.
Garba ka teesra din hai
aur group mein yahi
chal raha hai.
Dekho na,
gate pe ek guard hai,
ek register hai,
aur raat bhar log
aa rahe hain,
jaa rahe hain.
Register mein likha hai
'Rahul, guest'.
Ab kaunsa Rahul?
Kiske ghar?
Guard ko bhi
kya bolein yaar,
DJ itna loud hai,
wo kya poochega.
Aur agle din poora
WhatsApp group hi
deactivate ban jaata hai.
Aapki society mein
garba pe
bahar wale aate hain?
