# Shot list: "Desk from above" carousel

One long desk, shot from directly above, continuous across 10 slides. Swiping moves you along the desk.
About 20 minutes. Phone only.

## Setup (this matters more than the camera)
- **Surface:** one long table, or the floor with a plain sheet or chart paper. About 3 m long, so all props fit in one line from left to right.
- **Light:** daylight from a window on one side. No tube light and no flash. Turn the ceiling lights off.
- **Camera:** phone held flat, pointing straight down, at the **same height for every shot** (stand on a chair if needed). Use 1× lens. Don't use portrait mode.
- **Overlap:** shoot left to right. Each photo should overlap the previous one by about a quarter, so I can stitch them.
- **Don't move props between shots.**
